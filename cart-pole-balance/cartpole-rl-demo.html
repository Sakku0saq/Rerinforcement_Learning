How it works

Every episode runs through the same four-step loop. Nothing here is pre-trained — the policy starts from random weights and only gets better because bad attempts get thrown away.

01

Observe

Read the cart's position and velocity, and the pole's angle and angular velocity.

02

Act

A linear policy multiplies the observation by a weight vector and pushes the cart left or right.

03

Reward

Every timestep the pole stays upright is worth one point. The episode ends when it falls.

04

Update

The weights get a small random nudge. If the episode lasted longer, the nudge is kept — otherwise it's discarded.

The physics

The cart and pole follow the standard CartPole-v1 equations of motion — gravity, cart mass, pole mass and length all feed into the same second-order dynamics used in the classic control-theory literature, stepped forward 50 times a second.

The learning method

This is hill-climbing, sometimes called random search: the simplest policy-improvement method that still reliably solves CartPole. No gradients, no neural network — just propose, evaluate, and keep what works.

Built with vanilla JavaScript, Chart.js, and the CartPole-v1 dynamics from Gymnasium.
